Dear Dean Atwill and Marcela

I'm writing to formally confirm the change I've discussed with Marcela: this spring will be my last semester at NYU Shanghai.

This is a hard letter to write because I really loved my time here. IMA has been a creative and intellectual home for me; the students are extraordinary, my colleagues are dedicated and inspiring, and the opportunity I've had to be an educator in ways I believe in has been rare and precious. The past two years at IMA has shaped how I think about teaching, making, and building a strong, resilient, and future-facing community. I'm very grateful and I will miss it dearly.

Part of what shaped this decision was also the uncertainty around my FTCC position. Without a clear path for career development and support for arts research, it became hard to see a long-term future here as a practicing artist and researcher alongside being a full-time teaching faculty. I've reached a point where I need to follow my research with my full energy and attention, and I know I can't do that while fully committing to my responsibilities here.

I'm fully committed to seeing the semester through and I'm happy to help with the transition in any way that's useful. Thank you for your support and trust throughout my time here.

With love and gratitude,
Viola
